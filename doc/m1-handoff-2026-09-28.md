## Current blockers and evidence

### Main board power resistance and reset

- MB-005: current routed receipt is valid, but `python3
  hw/power/main_input_heat.py build/hw/main` fails I1–I4. The positive J1→F1→U2
  copper scenario is 73.060 mΩ at the modeled 115 °C corner, already 53.060
  mΩ above the entire assembled 20 mΩ loop limit, before ground/contact/solder.
  At 3.213 A it models 234.7 mV drop and 754 mW heat. Focused
  `python3 test/hw/test_main_input_heat.py` passed five checks.
- A two-sided F1/U2 relocation trial is not a candidate route: four opens and
  ten DRC findings. It models 16.600 mΩ positive and 2.250 mΩ single-plane
  return, before contacts and other transitions.
- The fitted HRO USB-C receptacle's published contact resistance does not
  guarantee the exact mated VBUS+GND groups at the project's current,
  temperature, life, and orientation conditions. The proposed ≤4 mΩ combined
  contact allocation is unqualified. Next dependency: exact mated connector
  pair guarantee/measurement or a product decision to choose another power
  interface. Only then can a qualified route target of ≤7/3/1 mΩ positive,
  return, transitions be engineered and verified. See
  `doc/hardware/main-mb005-connector-decision-20260928.md`.
- MB-051: all seven reset paths are copper-connected on the valid, zero-DRC
  main receipt, but the fitted MAX811T monitors only 3V3 and can release below
  the 3V3 valid floor; six slot resets can release while +1V2 is absent. A
  TPS3702 screen has only 2 mV/1 mV margins at SENSE before skew, tolerance,
  and route drops; stock is unverified. Need guaranteed load-side +1V2
  operating bounds and a supervisor architecture with margin, followed by
  fault/startup simulation. No speculative PCB change is justified.

### Cards and fabrication

  actual current clearance is 0.200 mm. Current fixed pad pitch and CEM notch
  dimensions cannot be corrected by a socket-only or notch-only change.
  Shifted/narrowed fingers require socket contact-wipe and fabricator
  registration guarantees. See `doc/hardware/card-notch-release-decision-20260928.md`
  and `doc/hardware/card-notch-qualification.md`.
- New `tools/fab_profile_containment.py` checks plotted copper and Excellon
