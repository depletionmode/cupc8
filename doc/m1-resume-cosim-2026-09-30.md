## SPI operating speed audit

The boot ROM slot probe and GPU, keyboard, storage and Wi-Fi drivers already
write SPI_CFG 16 (divider 2, mode 0), producing 3 MHz. They need no speed
reduction to match a 3 MHz routed SI case.

The raw IRQ fixture in `test/emu/cpu_irq_probe.mjs` replaces the fixed-ROM
entry before boot, writes device-0 SPI registers directly, and never writes
SPI_CFG. `soc/chipset.vhd` initializes configuration to zero, and
`soc/spi_master.vhd` treats divider zero as one: that test-only stimulus
runs at 6 MHz. It is used by copper IRQ and IO USB-host mutation probes.
The standalone GPU firmware test also deliberately tries every divider
1 through 31 through `SlotHost`, including 6 MHz. Those tests exercise
firmware/RTL behavior; digital co-sim does not establish analog SI at
that speed. Normal boot and user programs use the configured 3 MHz path.
